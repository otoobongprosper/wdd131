Site Name

PersonalExpense Tracker

Tagline: Take control of your money, one expense at a time.

The name PersonalExpense Tracker was chosen because it clearly communicates the main purpose of the website: helping individuals keep track of their personal spending. It is short, easy to remember, and directly relates to the service the website provides.

Logo Concept: The logo uses the letters "PET" inside a rounded square. The letters represent "PersonalExpense Tracker", while the simple design makes the logo easy to recognize at different screen sizes. It is positioned within the header and appears on all the pages.

Site Purpose

PersonalExpenseTracker is a personal finance website designed to help individuals record, organize, manage, and monitor their daily expenses in Nigerian naira (₦). It will provide a simple way for users to understand where their money goes and make better spending decisions.

Scenarios

These are questions that visitors from the target audience may ask when using the website:

  • How can I record my daily expenses and know how much I have spent this month?
  • Can I organize my expenses into categories such as food, transportation, bills, and savings?
  • How can I find or remove an expense that I entered incorrectly?
  • What practical steps can I take to reduce unnecessary spending and stay within my budget?

Target Audience: Students, young professionals, and individuals who want a straightforward way to monitor personal spending and develop better money-management habits.

Typography

The website will use two Google Fonts to maintain readability and a consistent visual appearance.

Roboto - Headings

Used for the site name, page titles, section headings, and other important labels. Its bold, clean appearance helps headings stand out.

sanrif — Body Text

Used for paragraphs, form labels, navigation links, buttons, and general content because it is clear and easy to read on both mobile and desktop screens.
